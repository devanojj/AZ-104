## What is Azure Policy?
Enforces **rules** on Azure resources to ensure **compliance** with organisational standards.
Policies are written in **JSON**.

---

## Azure Policy Initiative
A collection of **multiple policy definitions** grouped together for a common goal.

Used to:
- Simplify policy management
- Track compliance
- Apply multiple policies at once

---

## AZ-104 Exam Tips
- **Policy** = Single rule
- **Initiative** = Group of policies
- Policies are **JSON-based**
- Used for **governance and compliance**
