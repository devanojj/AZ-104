- **SSPR** = allows users to reset their own Microsoft Entra ID passwords.
- Reduces manual password-reset requests for administrators.

---
#### SSPR Requirements
- Requires **Microsoft Entra ID P1**.
- Requires a **paid subscription**.
- Free and pay-as-you-go subscriptions **cannot** use this feature.
---
#### Exam Facts
- Reduces **password-reset help desk requests**.
- Requires a **Microsoft Entra tenant**.
- **Authentication Policy Administrator** → can configure authentication policies such as SSPR.
