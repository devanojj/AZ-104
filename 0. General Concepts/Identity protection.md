**Microsoft Entra ID Protection** = detects and responds to **identity-related risks** such as compromised users or suspicious sign-ins.


- **Risky users** → user account may be compromised
- **Risky sign-ins** → suspicious login attempt
- **Risk detections** → leaked credentials, unusual locations, anonymous IPs, etc.


Identity Protection **detects** the risk. You can then use [[Conditional Access]] to decide what happens.
